# The Sensemaking Sprint (working title) — a co-design brief

**Status:** proposal for Pod Squad co-design — nothing here is a decree except the section marked "Fixed."
**Owner:** Brendan Whitaker · **Drafted:** July 2026 · **Lives:** this doc is the Pod Squad's to rewrite (PRs welcome; ✨ sprint-idea issues for anything you want to discuss first).

---

## What this event is

The first in-person experience after the Kickoff Summit — the room where the cohort's field evidence becomes shared maps. Participants arrive having distributed the field survey and (ideally) extracted their harvest into source-extract cards; they leave having sorted their pools, built their first Evidence on the canvas, climbed it into Patterns and Themes, and — for tables that move fast — mapped a first draft Problem Situation. It is the moment the cycle pivots from *collecting* to *making sense*.

It is **not** the day pods form, paradoxes get finished, or anything gets decided. Forcing convergence on day one is precisely the failure mode the method exists to prevent.

## Q1 — the name (yes, the name is co-design question #1)

This event slot has carried three names:

1. **Problem Sprint** — the name on the public calendar (July 28, DC Public Library) and in the Open Cycle Agreement's presence commitment.
2. **Paradox Sprint** — the owner's July design docs renamed it, on the argument that *"the paradox is the point."*
3. **Sensemaking Sprint** — the current working title, on the argument that participants at this event are nowhere near a paradox yet; what they're actually doing all day is *sensemaking*, and "problematization day" (an earlier internal name) was accurate but unlovable.

Should we name it for what happens in the room (sensemaking) or for what the whole arc is aiming at (the paradox)?

## Fixed (not up for co-design)

- **The calendar slot:** July 25, 2026, DC Public Library — Main branch (anchor event #2 of six; attendance is part of the presence commitment). *The format of the slot is Q2 below.*
- **The cohort:** ~40+ participants.
- **The instrument:** this tool, in the browser, on whatever devices we settle (Q7). It works offline; venue wifi is not a dependency.
- **The BYO-LLM rule:** no in-app AI, ever. Any AI assistance at the event is participants running copied prompts in their own models.
- **The survey precedes it:** the field survey is live from Kickoff; the sprint works whatever harvest exists by then.
- **The posture:** Poderators shepherd; nobody grades anything.

## The tool the room will run

The instrument was simplified in the run-up to the sprint. What matters for
facilitation:

- **It opens straight onto Sources.** There is no intro deck to skim. Whoever
  opens the session says what the tool is; the tool doesn't say it first.
- **The bottom action bar is the whole on-ramp.** One next move, and when it's
  locked, the one thing that unblocks it. The corner coach and the "Why this
  step?" panel are gone — *you* carry the why now, out loud. When someone
  stalls, read their bar with them.
- **The full arc is intact**, exports included. Nothing was cut for being "too
  far along for one morning."
- **Clearing a board takes a deliberate trip** to Share / Export → Start over.
  It is no longer a button next to the forward action.
- **The deck export names the stage that's blocking it** if the five Deepen
  stages aren't finished, and offers to take the person there.
